Our History & Industry Leadership

Established with a vision to bridge the digital skills gap in Abuja and across Nigeria, Jerro Computer School Limited stands as a pioneer in hands-on, practical ICT education. Beyond founding Jerro Computer School, our Managing Director is also the founder and Pioneer National Chairman of the Association of Computer Institute Operators of Nigeria (ACION)—an executive council uniting computer training institutes across all 36 states and the FCT.

Our Heritage

The History of JERRO COMPUTER SCHOOL

Founded with a vision to bridge the technological gap in Abuja, JERRO COMPUTER SCHOOL LIMITED started as a modest training center at Nysc camp Kubwa, abuja and has grown into a government-approved limited liability institution.

Over the years, we have trained hundreds of students, professionals, and NYSC corp members, equipping them with cutting-edge digital skills that match industry standards. Our commitment to excellence, integrity, and practical knowledge remains our strongest pillar.
